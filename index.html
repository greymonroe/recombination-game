Linkage & Recombination Lab

Two inbred parents were crossed to make an F1 plant. Self the F1, look at the offspring, and measure how often markers recombine.

The cross

The organism has two chromosomes (n = 2) carrying seven marker sites, A–D on chromosome 1 and E–G on chromosome 2. At each site the blue parent has one DNA base and the red parent has a different one, so the F1 is heterozygous at every site.

Recombination frequency and distance

Each dot is one pair of markers. Pairs on the same chromosome are placed by the distance between them; pairs on different chromosomes are grouped at the right.

Recombination frequencies are maximum-likelihood estimates from F2 genotype counts. Simulated crossovers follow a no-interference (Haldane) model.
