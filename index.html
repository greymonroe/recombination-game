Linkage & Recombination Lab

Two inbred parents were crossed to make an F1 plant. Testcross the F1 back to Parent 2, look at the offspring, and measure how often markers recombine.

The cross

The organism has two chromosomes (n = 2) carrying seven SNP markers, A–D on chromosome 1 and E–G on chromosome 2. At each marker Parent 1 has one DNA base and Parent 2 has a different one, so the F1 is heterozygous everywhere. The tester can only pass on Parent 2 chromosomes, so each offspring's genotype shows exactly which gamete the F1 made.

Recombination frequency is the share of testcross offspring carrying a recombinant F1 gamete. Simulated crossovers follow a no-interference (Haldane) model.
