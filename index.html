Linkage & Recombination Lab

Two inbred parents were crossed to make an F1 plant. Self the F1, look at the offspring, and measure how often markers recombine.

The cross

The organism has two chromosomes (n = 2) carrying seven markers, A–D on chromosome 1 and E–G on chromosome 2. The blue parent carries the uppercase allele at every marker and the orange parent carries the lowercase allele, so the F1 is heterozygous everywhere.
